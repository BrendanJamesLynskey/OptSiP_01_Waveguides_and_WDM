# Silicon Photonics Fundamentals 01 — Waveguides, Couplers and WDM

How a silicon wire guides light, and what the 220-nanometre platform makes cheap and what it makes hard: guided modes and group index, loss and bends, getting light on and off the chip, splitters and couplers, interferometers and rings, temperature and fabrication sensitivity, and the wavelength grids and multiplexers that put many channels on one fibre.

Topics: SOI, Guided modes, Group index, Grating couplers, Rings, WDM.

**Live site:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/OptSiP_01_Waveguides_and_WDM/

Part of the [Silicon Photonics Fundamentals series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/Optical_Hub_Silicon_Photonics). This is mostly a literature deck: every number is cited on the slide to a paper, standard or datasheet with a link, or computed live by the deck's interactive; vendor claims are labelled as the vendor's own and illustrative coefficients as illustrative. Every concept used here is explained, with links, in the [series glossary](https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/Optical_Hub_Silicon_Photonics/#glossary).
